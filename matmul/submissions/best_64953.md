## Method

This improves the merged 66,178 record by 1,225 read-cost units and
the pending 65,084 submission in [PR #50](https://github.com/cybertronai/sutro-problems/pull/50)
by 131. It builds on Juraj Selep's newest-first snake traversal and
JIT A staging, which in turn follows the earlier Cosmin and other contributors'
staging/allocation lineage. The PR50 seed is included with its provenance and hash
so the complete successful search can be replayed offline.

For rows 0–3 and columns 8–15, reverse column traversal in contraction passes
1, 3, 5, 7, and 9, in addition to the seed's pass 11. Reorder only along semantic SSA
dependencies, then optimize placement across pairs of address-cost tiers.
Each pair allocation is an integral min-cost-flow problem enforcing both tiers'
simultaneous live-value capacities. Seeded cost-neutral placement changes expose
further improving pair moves.

The arithmetic/copy DAG and operation counts are retained. Input placement,
read-before-write reuse, and all output reads at exit obey the existing scorer.
No global-optimality claim is made.
